**词性**
- **名词**，人，物品，抽象概念。学科数组。关系，表示类型，子类的词。同义词。
	- 名词，注意复数加s。主语，动词后，介词后，短语动词后。
- **动词**，加名词，加人，加物。也可以不加。短语动词，固定几个单词组成，有限的几个副词介词之一。动宾也有固定搭配。
	- 动词 动词加名词。短语动词（副词，介词），动词加动词不定式，加从句。注意有固定搭配。注意第三人称单数，注意时态。
- **形容词**，加名词。be加形容词加介词，相当于及物动词。
	- 形容词 系动词加形容词 ，形容词加名词。注意有固定搭配。系动词加形容词加介词，加动词加从句。颜色，形状。
- **副词**，动词后，句尾
	- 副词，位置，功能。
- **介词**，连词较为固定。动词，名词，形容词后面可以加介词。
- 其他词性相当于以上。比如介词短语相当于形容词或副词。代词相当于名词。
- 有的单词多个词性，比如动词当动词用。通过词尾改变词性。有的不及物动词可以做及物动词，表示使得。

注意一些意思相关的单词用法相同。在短语中理解单词的意思。

- **长句**，非谓语动词，从句，并列句。
	- 结尾可以补充，开头可以引起注意或者延长思考时间，中间可以插入。
	- 长主语。定语从句，非谓语动词，介词短语，主语从句。
	- 句尾可以有定语从句，状语从句中可以嵌套定语从句。
	- 句子开头，连接词，引导宾语从句。

- **长句**是因为**先概括再具体**。在这个句末不断地补充，或者是这个句首有个长状语或者是引导。

English Grammar Checklist 
一、词法 Word‑level Grammar
1. Nouns 名词
1. countable & uncountable nouns 可数&不可数
I have two books. (countable) / There is some water. (uncountable)
2. plural forms 名词复数
There are many boxes on the table.
3. possessive nouns ('s / of) 名词所有格
This is Tom’s bag. / the door of the room
2. Pronouns 代词
1. personal pronouns 人称代词（主格/宾格）
She likes him. (subject) / I know her. (object)
2. possessive pronouns 物主代词（形容词性 / 名词性）
This is my pen. / This pen is mine.
3. reflexive pronouns 反身代词
He hurt himself.
4. indefinite pronouns 不定代词 (some, any, no, every‑)
Someone is knocking at the door. / I don’t have any time.
3. Adjectives 形容词
1. order of adjectives 形容词排序
a beautiful small old wooden box
2. comparative & superlative 比较级、最高级
He is taller than me. / She is the tallest girl in class.
4. Adverbs 副词
1. adverb formation & position 副词构成与位置
He runs quickly.
2. adverb comparative & superlative 副词比较最高级
Tom speaks English better than Jack.
5. Verbs 动词
1. main / linking / auxiliary / modal verbs 实义/系/助/情态动词
She looks happy. (linking) / I can swim. (modal)
2. regular & irregular verbs 规则&不规则动词
work‑worked‑worked / go‑went‑gone
6. Prepositions 介词
1. prepositions of time (in / on / at) 时间介词
We have class in the morning, on Monday, at 8:00.
2. prepositions of place 地点介词
The cat is under the chair.
3. prepositional collocations 介词固定搭配
I am good at English.
7. Conjunctions 连词
1. coordinating conjunctions 并列连词(FANBOYS)
I want to go, but I am busy.
2. subordinating conjunctions 从属连词
I will stay home because it rains.
二、Tenses 时态
1. Simple Present 一般现在时
He gets up at seven every day.
2. Simple Past 一般过去时
I visited Beijing last year.
3. Simple Future (will / be going to) 一般将来时
It is going to rain. / I will call you.
4. Present Continuous 现在进行时
They are playing football now.
5. Past Continuous 过去进行时
I was reading books at 8 last night.
6. Present Perfect 现在完成时
I have finished my homework.
7. Present Perfect Continuous 现在完成进行时
It has been raining for two hours.
8. Past Perfect 过去完成时
When I arrived, he had left.
9. Past Future 过去将来时
He said he would come.
三、Voice 语态
Passive voice 被动语态
Many trees are planted every spring.
The letter was written by Mary yesterday.
四、Non‑finite verbs 非谓语动词
1. to‑infinitive (to do) 不定式
I want to sleep.
2. gerund (doing) 动名词
I enjoy reading.
3. participles: doing / done 现在分词 & 过去分词
The running boy is my brother. / I saw a broken window.
五、Sentence Syntax 句法
8. Sentence types 句子类型
1. five basic sentence patterns 五大简单句
She smiles.（主谓）/ I love apples.（主谓宾）
2. compound sentences 并列句
Hurry up, or you will be late.
3. complex sentences 复合句
I know that he is honest.
9. Clauses 从句
1. Noun clauses 名词性从句（主、宾、表、同位语）
What he said is true.（主语从句）
I believe that he is right.（宾语从句）
2. Relative / Attributive clauses 定语从句
The girl who is singing is my sister.
This is the house where I lived.
3. Adverbial clauses 状语从句（时间/条件/原因/让步等）
I will go if it is sunny.（条件）
Although he is poor, he is happy.（让步）
10. Questions 疑问句
1. yes‑no questions 一般疑问句
Do you like coffee?
2. wh‑questions 特殊疑问句
Where do you live?
3. alternative questions 选择疑问句
Do you like tea or coffee?
4. tag questions 反义疑问句
You like music, don’t you?
11. Negation 否定句
I do not know him.
12. Imperatives 祈使句
Open the door.
13. Exclamations (what / how) 感叹句
What a nice day! / How beautiful she is!
六、Special Structures 特殊句式
1. Inversion 倒装（部分、完全倒装）
Never have I seen such a place.（部分倒装）
2. Emphatic structure It is / was … that … 强调句
It was Tom that helped me.
3. Subjunctive mood 虚拟语气
If I were you, I would take the chance.
4. There be 句型
There is a book on the desk.
5. Subject‑verb agreement 主谓一致
The group is small. / The group are arguing.
七、Common Mistakes 高频易错
1. Articles a / an / the / zero article 冠词
I saw a dog. The dog was black. / I like music.（零冠词）
2. Modal verbs 情态动词辨析
You must finish it. / You should rest.
3. Determiners some‑any‑many‑much‑few‑little 限定词
There is little milk left.
4. Direct & Indirect Speech 直接引语&间接引语
He said: “I am tired.” → He said (that) he was tired.
