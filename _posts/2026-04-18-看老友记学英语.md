### **动词**
- **不及物动词**（后面不直接接宾语）： the phone starts to ring. We used to work together.
- **及物动词**（后面直接接宾语）： I'll have a cup of coffee. I'm gonna order a pizza.
- **双宾语**（动词 + 人 + 物 = 间接宾语 + 直接宾语；wish + 人 + 名词，表祝愿）： He wants to give me a Mercedes convertible. I'll buy you a bagel. Wish me luck!
- 动词+ **宾语 + 补语**（宾补补充说明宾语）： You can just put it on the table. We want you to be happy. leave + 宾语 + 形容词： No, just leave my aura alone, okay?
- **系动词**（+ 表语构成系表结构，表状态或身份；be 后接名词/形容词/介词短语，become/look/feel 也是）： I'm in Las Vegas. I'm Liza Minnelli. 


- **情态动词**
	- 义务/必要： have to 不得不： you have to take a course. I have to go to China. you don't have to fly back. must 必须： You must stop!
	- 过去习惯： used to： We used to work together.
	- 应该/建议： should（+ be doing，本不该）： She should not be wearing those pants. had better 最好： I'd better answer it.
	- 推测： must（gotta be）一定： There's gotta be something wrong with him! can't be 不可能： That can't be good.
	- 委婉： would： I would have to say that is an 'L'-shaped bracket. That'd be good. Would I have seen you in anything?（过去经历，委婉问）
	- 按理应该： supposed to： Well, I was kinda supposed to be headed for Aruba on my honeymoon, so nothing!

**时态**
- **一般现在时（do）**（表习惯性动作、客观事实、当前状态；主语三单时动词加 -s）： I never lie. I make the best oatmeal raisin cookies in the world. This is great.
- **过去时（did）**： No, I just talked to them.  I thought we had them. We already knew that. when you were a kid, ... I didn't tell anybody that I knew you. I can't believe you two had sex in her dream. because I told him I was 22. We just finished this magnificent Thanksgiving dinner. (just + 过去时，不要误写成 have done)
- **完成时（have done）**： Have you never done this *before*? Why have I never tasted these before? How long would you say Chandler's been in the shower? I've never looked at you that way before. Yeah, I think I've heard of that. I mean, I have never made coffee before in my entire life. We haven't known each other for that long a time. I haven't been able to, uh, perform.（have been able to：一直没能…）
	- **现在完成进行时（have been doing）**（动作从过去持续到现在，可能仍在进行）： I've been thinking......about how I'm always seeing girls on top of girls. I realize you guys have been wondering what exactly happened between Carol and me.
	- **过去完成时（had done）**（表"过去的过去"：在过去某一时刻之前已完成的动作）： My mom had just killed herself. By my sixth date with Paolo, he had already named both my breasts!
- **将来时（be going to do, will）**（表打算或预测；口语中 be going to 缩合为 gonna）：  I'm gonna get my stuff.  I'm gonna order a pizza. She is gonna hate me. You're gonna love it! Come on, they're gonna love you.
- **现在进行时（be doing）**（表正在进行的动作；口语也用它加强语气：I'm telling you 我跟你说）：No, I'm telling you last night was like umm, all my birthdays, both graduations, plus the barn raising scene in Witness.
	- **过去进行时（was/were doing）**（表过去某一时刻正在进行的动作）： Carol was wearing boots just like those the night that we- we first- y'know. I was just saying to my friend that I thought you were the most beautiful woman that I'd ever seen. I was looking at this gravy boat.

**语态**
- 主动：默认语态，剧中大多数句子，如 I'm gonna order a pizza.
- 被动(be + 过去分词；口语常用 get + 过去分词表被动)：  I was stuck at work. I got fired. Chandler got bitten by the peacock at the zoo.

**非谓语动词**
- **不定式 to do**
    - **动词+不定式**，是最基本的用法：All of a sudden, the phone starts to ring.
	- 动词+疑问词+不定式：I don't know what to say, Ross. Uh, it's a monkey.  Now I don't know what to do, everybody starts looking at me. 疑问词+不定式
　　　- 表目的作为状语： Mom already called this morning to remind me not to wear my hair up. Joey and Chandler are coming over to help me put together my new furniture.

　　- be + 形容词 + 不定式： Are we ready to play some serious poker?
	- 名词 + 不定式：Well, at least she had the chance to leave a man at the altar. 名词+不定式（名词后）
		- 名词 + 不定式（不定式 to do 作定语，放在名词后）： There's only one way to resolve this. There's nothing to tell!
		- enough + 不定式（足够…去做）： Even if I could get it together enough to ask a woman out...
	- for + 人 + to do（给不定式加上动作的发出者）：I cannot wait for you to meet my friends.
- **动名词 doing**
	- **动词+doing**：Stop cleansing my aura! Joey. Stop hitting on her! keep doing： Why does everyone keep fixating on that? try doing（试着做）： you might try accidentally breaking something valuable of hers.
	- 介词+doing，做状语： She sits down without saying hi. Instead of putting her information, I put mine.

	- 独立主格（with + 宾语 + 介词短语/分词，作伴随状语；口语中少见）： with a hanger in your mouth. with the pin in it.

	- 现在分词（短语）作定语，修饰前面的名词，表主动/正在进行：He's like actually some spy, working for the C.I.A.（一个给中央情报局工作的间谍） Well, you ladies are not the only ones living the dream. It's just two people going out to dinner and- not having sex.

短语（按照语法）：
- 短语动词 look for, talk about, look at, be mad at, be up to, live off, take credit for, steer clear of, have a crush on, 
- kind of 作为程度副词
- 名词： credit card
- 语气词 : well, y'know, I mean, like, Oh, Wow, Alright
- 口语缩合：gonna (= going to), wanna (= want to), gotta (= got to), kinda (= kind of), outta (= out of), lemme (= let me), coupla (= couple of), gimme (= give me), c'mere (= come here), how'd (= how did), what're (= what are), that'd (= that would), ya (= you), 'cause (= because), 'n' (= and)

- **礼貌用语**

	- 祝福： Good luck. Congratulations.

	- 问候： Hey, you guys.
